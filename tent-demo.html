Table tent demo

You are looking down at a dinner table. A Samsung Galaxy A08 lies on top of the pile of phones with the paper tent over it. Its screen lights the printed circles through the paper.

  • Click “who’s next”. A light runs along the names and stops on one.
  • Watch the row of dots: one lights every five minutes of the meal. Here, five minutes pass every three seconds.
  • Pick up a phone to end the meal.
The meal has started Nothing on the tent shows a clock or a number. This box is only here for the demo.

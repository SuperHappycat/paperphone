Window demo

An iPhone with its case turned around, so the case covers the screen. The only part you can see or touch is the opening meant for the cameras.

  • Drag sideways inside the window, or use the buttons, to change what it shows.
  • Click inside the window to use it: start the timer, take a photo, move to the next step, wake the face.

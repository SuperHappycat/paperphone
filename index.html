Paper Phone

Pick what you need for the day, print one sheet, fold it three times, and leave your phone at home.

A paper sleeve for your phone. A keypad is printed over the screen and glows through the paper, so the phone only makes calls. The back carries your contacts or plans.

Try the envelope on this computer

On the phone, open the keypad page and press Start before sliding it into the envelope. If the printed circles sit above or below the glowing ones, change “Screen starts” by that many millimetres and print again.

Contacts

    Up to 7 people. An imported address book is only read in this browser tab and is not saved.

    Calendar

    The first 6 events are printed. Importing adds the events that fall on the day chosen above; export an .ics file from Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

    Tasks

    Up to 9 tasks. Empty circles are added so you can write more by hand.

    Photo

    A ticket, a screenshot, a face you like. It is printed in black and white; wide pictures are turned sideways to fill the panel.

    Notes
    Weather

    Forecast by Open-Meteo. No account or key needed.

    Paper app
    Map and directions

    Printed on the back of the sheet, so print double sided (flip on the long edge). Map data from OpenStreetMap.

    Card slot

    Adds four dotted cut lines around the paper app panel. Cut along them and the folded phone holds a bank or transit card.

    Everything you type stays in this browser. A web version of Paper Phone by Special Projects, Apache 2.0.

    Folding your Paper Phone

    1. Lay the sheet printed side down (map side up, if you printed one). Fold it in half like closing a book, so the print is on the outside.
    2. Fold the top edge down to the bottom edge, so the two small black squares meet on the inside.
    3. Fold in half once more, with your name on the front cover.

    If you added the card slot, cut along the four dotted lines before folding and tuck your card into the corners.

    For the phone envelope, the steps are printed on the sheet itself.

    When printing, choose 100% scale (not “fit to page”) and no headers or footers. The booklet prints landscape, the envelope portrait.

    Start over?

    This clears your name, contacts, events, tasks, picture and route from this browser. It cannot be undone.

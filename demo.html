Envelope demo

This is a Samsung Galaxy A08 inside its paper envelope, drawn to scale. The keys are lit by the phone screen underneath and show through the paper. Click them as you would tap the envelope.

  • Click digits to dial. You hear the dial tones.
  • Click the large circle to call. Hold it to clear the number.
  • Click the lowest circle and watch the keypad flash the time, one digit at a time.

Envelope keypad

Open this page on the phone that goes in the envelope. Press Start, slide the phone into the envelope screen first, and the keys glow through the paper.

Tap digits to dial, then the large circle to call. Hold the large circle to clear the number. The lower circle flashes the time on the keypad, digit by digit.

Calling hands the number to your phone’s own dialler, which may ask for one more tap on its call button. A web page is not allowed to place the call itself.

Do this before the paper goes on. Nothing should ask you anything once the phone is in the envelope.

    The first call can make the phone ask which app should dial. Try it now and choose “Always”, then come back to this page.
